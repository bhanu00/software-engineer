# Observability

Observability combines logs, metrics, and traces to explain a system's internal state from its outputs.

## Study sequence

1. Review [cloud monitoring](../4-Cloud-and-DevOps/1-devops/monitoring-cloud-based.md).
2. Compare [ELK-based monitoring](../4-Cloud-and-DevOps/1-devops/monitoring-elk-without-public-cloud.md).
3. Apply the signals to the [system-design basics](../5-System-Design/1-system-design-basics/README.md) and define service-level indicators before choosing dashboards or alerts.

For operational practices, continue with [operational excellence](../4-Cloud-and-DevOps/1-devops/operational-excellence.md).
