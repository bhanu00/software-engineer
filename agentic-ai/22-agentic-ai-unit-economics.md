# Agentic AI Unit Economics

## Scope

Model, retrieval, compute, storage, observability, and human-review costs.

## Topics

- Cost per request, task, workflow, tenant, and business outcome.
- Input/output tokens, model routing, caching, batching, and prompt compression.
- Retrieval, storage, queues, compute, observability, and human operations.
- Budget ownership, quotas, alerts, and cost SLOs.
- Capacity, marginal cost, and value realization.

## Architect questions

- What is the fully loaded cost of a successful task?
- How does cost change with retries, long context, or human review?
- Which optimization reduces spend without damaging quality or safety?
