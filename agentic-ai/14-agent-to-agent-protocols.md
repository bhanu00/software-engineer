# Agent-to-Agent Protocols

## Scope

A2A-style handoffs, capability discovery, identity, trust, and protocol versioning.

## Topics

- Agent identity, capabilities, task requests, status, and results.
- Handoff contracts, context boundaries, and evidence transfer.
- Trust establishment, authorization, and delegated permissions.
- Asynchronous tasks, retries, cancellation, and expiration.
- Compatibility, versioning, and observability across agents.

## Architect questions

- How does one agent verify another agent's identity and authority?
- What information is safe to transfer across the handoff?
- Who owns errors and side effects after delegation?
