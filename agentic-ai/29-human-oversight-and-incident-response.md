# Human Oversight and AI Incident Response

## Scope

Explanations, appeals, overrides, safe shutdown, and incident response.

## Topics

- Meaningful explanations and evidence for important decisions.
- Appeals, correction, human override, and escalation paths.
- Safe shutdown, kill switches, rollback, and containment.
- Harmful output, data leakage, unauthorized action, and provider outage response.
- Incident roles, communications, evidence preservation, and post-incident learning.

## Architect questions

- Who can stop the system and who can authorize restart?
- How are affected users notified and supported?
- How does each incident improve controls, evaluations, and governance?
