# AI Governance Operating Model

## Scope

Ownership, model inventories, risk classification, approvals, exceptions, and audit evidence.

## Topics

- AI use-case intake and risk classification.
- Roles for product, engineering, security, legal, privacy, and operations.
- Model, prompt, tool, dataset, and vendor inventories.
- Approval gates, evidence retention, exceptions, and decommissioning.
- Monitoring, incident response, and accountability.

## Architect questions

- Who owns the system and its residual risk?
- What must be approved before production use?
- Can an auditor reconstruct the decisions and controls?
