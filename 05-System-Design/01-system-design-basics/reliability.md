# Reliability

Placeholder for correctness over time, failure rates, durability, recovery objectives, and methods for measuring and improving dependable system behavior.

Reliability asks whether a system continues to produce correct results over a period of time. Define the expected workload and failure conditions before selecting redundancy or recovery mechanisms.
