# System Design Interview Practice

Use these prompts after reviewing [how to approach HLD](../../5-System-Design/1-system-design-basics/how-to-approach-hld.md). For each problem, clarify requirements, estimate workload, define APIs and data, sketch the design, discuss trade-offs, and cover failure handling and operations.
