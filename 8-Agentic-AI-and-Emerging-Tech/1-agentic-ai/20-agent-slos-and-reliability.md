# Agent SLOs and Reliability

## Scope

SLOs, graceful degradation, circuit breakers, provider fallback, capacity, and incident response.

## Topics

- SLOs for task success, availability, latency, safety, and cost.
- Error budgets and release decisions for nondeterministic systems.
- Timeouts, retries, circuit breakers, fallbacks, and degraded modes.
- Capacity planning for tokens, concurrency, queues, and provider limits.
- Incident detection, response, recovery, and post-incident learning.

## Architect questions

- What does availability mean when an answer can still be wrong?
- Which failures degrade gracefully and which require a hard stop?
- How are reliability and quality ownership assigned?
