# Agent Quality Drift and Hallucination Monitoring

## Scope

Hallucination, groundedness, retrieval drift, model behavior changes, and workflow outcome monitoring.

## Topics

- Unsupported claims, citation errors, and abstention behavior.
- Retrieval quality, document freshness, and corpus drift.
- Model, prompt, tool, and provider behavior changes.
- Online feedback, human review, outcome metrics, and alerts.
- Investigation, rollback, retraining, re-indexing, and corrective action.

## Architect questions

- How do you distinguish model error from source-data error?
- Which drift signals are leading indicators?
- What is the rollback or remediation path when quality declines?
