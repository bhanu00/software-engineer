# AI Risk Frameworks and Regulation

## Scope

NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act concepts, privacy, accessibility, records management, and sector controls.

## Topics

- Govern, map, measure, and manage activities from NIST AI RMF.
- Risk-based obligations and human oversight concepts.
- Privacy, accessibility, records, and sector-specific requirements.
- Evidence, transparency, documentation, and accountability.
- Translating principles into engineering controls and review gates.

## Architect questions

- Which regulations and policies apply to the use case and region?
- What evidence demonstrates compliance rather than merely intent?
- How do legal requirements affect architecture and lifecycle management?
