# Agent Quality Dimensions

## Scope

Task success, groundedness, safety, latency, cost, and human-review rate.

## Topics

- Functional task success and output correctness.
- Groundedness, evidence quality, citation correctness, and abstention.
- Safety, policy compliance, fairness, and unauthorized-action rate.
- Latency, availability, throughput, and user experience.
- Cost per task, human-review rate, and rework.

## Architect questions

- Which quality dimensions are most important for this use case?
- What trade-offs are acceptable and who approves them?
- Are metrics measured offline, online, or both?
