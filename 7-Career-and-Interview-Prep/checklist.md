# Career and Interview Preparation Checklist

Use this checklist to track demonstrated skills, not just pages read. Add examples or links beside each item as evidence.

## Engineering foundations

- [ ] Solve common data-structure and algorithm problems and explain complexity.
- [ ] Model a domain and justify object-oriented design decisions.
- [ ] Build and test a small application in a language or framework relevant to the role.

## System design and operations

- [ ] Clarify functional requirements, constraints, and non-functional requirements.
- [ ] Design APIs, data models, caches, and asynchronous workflows.
- [ ] Explain consistency, retries, idempotency, and dependency failure handling.
- [ ] Describe deployment, observability, security, and recovery trade-offs.
- [ ] Complete a case study and a portfolio-quality implementation or design document.

## Interviews and leadership

- [ ] Practice coding, system-design, and behavioral interviews on a regular cadence.
- [ ] Prepare evidence-based stories about technical decisions, influence, and outcomes.
- [ ] Explain a design to engineering, product, and executive audiences.
- [ ] Review the [target-role competency matrix](career/target-role-competency-matrix.md) and close the largest gaps.

Continue using the [career preparation plan](career/preparation-plan.md) to turn remaining gaps into weekly actions.
