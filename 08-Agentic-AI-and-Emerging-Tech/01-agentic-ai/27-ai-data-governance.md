# AI Data Governance

## Scope

Data classification, PII/PHI/PCI handling, residency, encryption, retention, deletion, and lineage.

## Topics

- Data classification and allowed model/provider usage.
- PII, PHI, PCI, secrets, and sensitive business data handling.
- Encryption in transit and at rest, residency, and private networking.
- Retention, deletion, correction, and subject-access requirements.
- Data lineage, provenance, consent, access control, and auditability.

## Architect questions

- What data enters prompts, retrieval indexes, traces, and model training?
- Can data be deleted from every derived store?
- How are residency and provider boundaries enforced?
